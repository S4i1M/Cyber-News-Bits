About Us

Cybersecurity enthusiasts dedicated to promoting online safety — one byte at a time.

Empowering minds with cyber & tech news

Cyber News Bits started as a passion project — a small team of security enthusiasts who believed that staying safe online shouldn't require a computer science degree.

Today we run a live news feed that pulls real-time threat intelligence from trusted sources like The Hacker News, BleepingComputer and Hacker News — automatically, with no ads, no tracking, and no sign-ups.

Our mission is simple: raise awareness about cyber threats, translate technical jargon into plain language, and help everyone make informed decisions to protect their digital lives.
